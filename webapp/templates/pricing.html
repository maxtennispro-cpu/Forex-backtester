Frequently asked questions

What exactly am I subscribing to?

Access to the analytics dashboard for our systematic FX strategy — its live backtest results, risk metrics, and (on Premium) the full trade log. We show research output; we do not manage money or place trades for you.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Plans are month-to-month with no lock-in; downgrading takes effect at the end of the billing period.

Is past performance a guarantee?

No. All results shown are simulated backtests. They include realistic costs but cannot include everything live trading involves, and future results will differ.
